I am a PhD student in machine learning for molecular biology at the [Department of Robotics, Perception, and Learning (RPL)](https://www.kth.se/rpl/department-of-robotics-perception-and-learning-1.779439) at [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://www.kth.se/en) under the supervision of Associate Professor [Hossein Azizpour](https://www.csc.kth.se/~azizpour/). I am also co-supervised by Associate Professor [Joakim Andén](https://www.kth.se/profile/janden?l=en) at the [KTH Department of Mathematics](https://www.kth.se/math/department-of-mathematics-1.833813) and [Marta Carroni](https://www.scilifelab.se/researchers/marta-carroni/), Platform Director of Cellular and Molecular Imaging and Head of the [Swedish National Cryo-EM Facility](https://cryoem.scilifelab.se/) at [SciLifeLab](https://www.scilifelab.se) and the [Department of Biochemistry and Biophysics](https://www.su.se/english/divisions/department-of-biochemistry-and-biophysics) at [Stockholm University (SU)](https://www.su.se).

Before starting my PhD, I recieved a [BSc in Engineering Mathematics](https://www.kth.se/utbildning/civilingenjor/teknisk-matematik/teknisk-matematik-civilingenjor-300-hp-1.923907) (GPA 4.8/5.0) and [MSc in Applied and Computational Mathematics](https://www.kth.se/en/studies/master/applied-and-computational-mathematics/msc-applied-and-computational-mathematics-1.344221) (GPA 4.95/5.00) at KTH, completing an exchange semester at the [Department of Mathematics](https://math.ethz.ch/) at [ETH Zürich](https://ethz.ch/en.html) (GPA 5.6/6.0) and conducting my MSc thesis on using diffusion models as priors for denoising of synthetic cryo-em images under Joakim Andén.

My research focuses on generative foundational models over flexible 3D macromolecular stucture as priors for downstream heteregeneous cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM) reconstruction. My work is part of [Digital Futures'](https://www.digitalfutures.kth.se/) flagship project on [recovering the dynamics of biological macromolecules using cryo-EM data with data-driven priors](https://www.digitalfutures.kth.se/project/recovering-the-dynamics-of-biological-macromolecules-using-cryo-em-data-with-data-driven-prior/), connecting Marta and [her group](https://www.su.se/english/research/research-catalogue/research-groups/a/marta-carronis-research-group)’s domain expertise and data curation capabilities at SU and SciLifeLab, Hossein and his group’s expertise in data-driven generative modeling in life science applications, and Joakim’s knowledge in statistical inverse problems and 3D reconstruction methods in cryo-EM.

More broadly, I am interested in all things machine learning and AI, in particular with applications in the sciences. This includes geometric deep learning, with for instance SE(3)-invariant and -equivariant models for molecules and proteins; generative modeling, such as diffusion and flow matching; and 3D and 4D representations of flexible volumes, including implicit neural representations, for reconstruction and generation. I am also drawn to the application of the aformentioned method for (Bayesian) inverse problems, where a learned prior has to be reconciled with noisy, indirect measurements, as in cryo-EM through for example posterior sampling.
